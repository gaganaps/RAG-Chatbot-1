# Problem Statement: Mutual Fund FAQ Assistant (Facts-Only Q&A)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of this project is to develop a lightweight, trustworthy, and compliant **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)-based Mutual Fund FAQ Assistant**, modeled within the product context of **Groww**. 

The assistant is engineered to resolve objective, verifiable mutual fund queries by retrieving answers strictly from official public sources—specifically Asset Management Company (AMC) official documentation, the Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI), and the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

> **Core Philosophy**: **Accuracy Over Intelligence.**  
> The system strictly refuses subjective investment advice, market speculation, and comparative performance judgments. Every response must be verifiable, concise, and backed by a single authoritative citation.

---

## 2. Product Context & Motivation

* **Reference Product**: Groww (Mutual Funds Investment Platform)
* **Domain**: Indian Mutual Fund Ecosystem (Regulated by SEBI & AMFI)
* **Problem**: Retail investors often struggle to find clear, authoritative factual answers (such as exit loads, expense ratios, lock-in terms, and taxation guidelines) scattered across lengthy Scheme Information Documents (SIDs) and Key Information Memorandums (KIMs). Concurrently, traditional AI chatbots tend to hallucinate or cross into unauthorized investment advice.
* **Solution**: A deterministic, guarded RAG pipeline delivering short, facts-only answers with official source references while maintaining zero-advice regulatory compliance.

### 4.2 Assistant Capabilities & Query Categories
The assistant answers **objective, facts-only questions**, including:
* **Fund Management & Managers**: Name(s) of designated fund managers, tenure (managing since date), educational/professional background, and other schemes managed by the same team.
* **Expense Ratios**: Total Expense Ratio (TER) of Regular vs. Direct plans.
* **Exit Loads**: Charges applicable on redemption within defined time windows.
* **SIP & Investment Limits**: Minimum initial investment and subsequent SIP increments.
* **Lock-in Periods**: Tax-saving schemes (ELSS) statutory 3-year lock-in.
* **Riskometer & Objectives**: Scheme risk classification and primary investment goal.
* **Benchmark Indices**: Assigned benchmark index (e.g., NIFTY 50 TRI, S&P BSE 500 TRI).
* **Operational Processes**: Step-by-step procedures to fetch statements, tax forms, or capital gains reports.

## 5. Strict Response Constraints & Formatting

Every answer generated by the assistant must strictly comply with the following structural rules:

```
[Concise factual answer (Maximum 3 sentences)]

Source: [Single Official Citation Link]
Last updated from sources: <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

1. **Length Limit**: Maximum of **3 sentences** per response.
2. **Single Citation Link**: Exactly **one valid, official source URL** per response (no multiple links, no aggregator sites).
3. **Mandatory Footer**: Must contain the recency indicator:
   `Last updated from sources: <date>`

---

## 6. Guardrails, Refusal Handling & Compliance

### 6.1 Refusal of Advisory & Opinion Queries
The system must actively detect and refuse any subjective, predictive, or advisory requests.

* **Trigger Query Examples**:
  * *"Should I invest in this fund?"*
  * *"Which fund is better: Fund A or Fund B?"*
  * *"Will this fund give 15% returns next year?"*
  * *"Is this fund manager good or should I avoid them?"* (Subjective manager ratings are refused; only factual data like manager names and tenure are provided)
* **Refusal Protocol**:
  1. Maintain a polite, objective, non-preachy tone.
  2. Explicitly state the facts-only scope of the assistant.
  3. Provide an official educational link (e.g., AMFI Investor Education or SEBI Investor Portal).

### 6.2 Performance & Returns Queries
* Direct calculation or comparative analysis of past returns is **prohibited**.
* For queries specifically regarding fund performance or historical returns, the assistant must direct the user to the official factsheet via direct link without subjective interpretation.

### 6.3 Privacy & Data Security (PII Safeguards)
Under no circumstance should the assistant ask for, accept, process, or store Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or sensitive financial credentials:
* **Prohibited Data**:
  * Permanent Account Number (PAN)
  * Aadhaar Number
  * Bank Account / Demat Account Numbers
  * One-Time Passwords (OTPs) / PINs
  * Personal contact info (Phone numbers, Personal Email addresses)
